# Surface brief: Wickwager (every game screen)

**Scope.** The whole game, built from `src/` into one HTML file (`index.html`). The visual source of truth is the Ember Lit mockup in `mockups/ember-lit/` (its `NOTES.md`, `lit.css`, `art.js`, `scene.js`, `play.js`). It covers:
- the title (the candle wordmark) and run setup (Deck, Stake, seed);
- Blind select, the play table, the payout, the cash-out, the Shop and open Booster Packs;
- Overview, the deck view, Options, Pause, How to play, Records and the Collection;
- Game Over, You win! and Endless mode.

**Mode.** Experience (the player is inside the game), held to Operate discipline for every number and control.

**Audience and job.** A player who loves the genre and wants a run that feels tense and readable, with scoring that pays off. They read the target, Blind score, Chips × Mult, hands, discards and money at a glance, then choose cards and play.

**Constraints.**
- The genre's game design and system words, played straight; the look and the items are the game's own (ARCH.md section 2 has the originality line, and DESIGN.md's Don'ts the looks it never uses).
- Pixel rules: sprites drawn from grids into bitmaps at 1:1, shown at one whole-number scale per breakpoint (`--px` 3 CSS px; 2 below 1024 px wide, where the compact layout takes over, and on a window under 870 px tall) with `image-rendering: pixelated`; never smoothed, rotated at rest, or drawn at two sizes on one screen. Light, glow, shadow and depth are separate layers.
- One animation engine (CSS and WAAPI), one `GAME_SPEED`; `?test=1` zeroes every duration; reduced motion keeps fades only.
- The owner's rejected looks and their reasons stand (PRODUCT.md): no casino cues, never too dark or one colour, never plain cards, scoring always juicy.
- One HTML file for desktop and phone (360 to 430 px portrait), no network.

**Memorable moment.** The payout: the plaque dropping on the bouncy spring in a burst of light.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The approved Ember pixel card table, relit as a premium 2026 indie game. A candlelit room in three depths (a far room of candles out of focus, pixel haze, embers rising), crisp pixel cards and Jokers, translucent plum panels with a warm lip of light, numbers that glow in their own colour, damped springs for everything that lands.

OWN-WORLD:
- **Ground.** Bands from plum to burnt orange (`#2C1535` to `#C65A26`); a hearth glow from below and a pool of candlelight over the stage; the edges fall off into warm plum, never black.
- **Colour jobs.** Chips blue `#4A62FF`; Mult red `#E9382B`; money flat yellow `#FFCE3B` with one `#D18A1D` rim (never a gold ramp); ember `#FF7A2B` for the primary action (Play hand, the Blind select's Play, Collect $N, Next Blind); pink `#FF4C9B` for Discard, Reroll and the Joker rack; lime `#9EE03C` for the Blind score and good news; violet `#8B46EE` for consumables, Tarot and the Boss Blind; cream card stock `#FFF3E0`; ink `#2B1220`. Every shadow is tinted plum.
- **Chrome.** 9-slice pixel panels on a 3-px stepped curve, a two-art-pixel lit lip along the top, fill temperature by job (the Blind warmer, the Blind score deeper, the racks lighter). Pills for labels and tags (the Chips and Mult pills sit one step deeper on their ramps, `#3346D6` and `#C8262F`, so their light ink keeps 4.5:1); buttons with a lit face, a highlight row and a lip that sinks when pressed. A label that sits on the open room rides on a small plum pill. The pressed segment of a toggle group (Sort, Game speed, Motion) sinks in yellow and carries a one-art-pixel dark bar under its label, as the chosen tab does, so it never relies on hue. Keyboard focus is a two-tone ring: pale yellow `#FFF0A0` outside, a band of the ink `#2B1220` inside, so one of the two clears 3:1 on cream stock, the lit floor or a plum panel; a focused card comes up over its neighbours, and everything that scrolls inside itself (a dialog body, the compact Joker rack, the Shop's shelves and rows, a pack's options, the run setup's Deck and Stake) keeps room for the ring inside its scroll edge, six pixels in most, so no ring is cut; the keyboard brings a control such a box shows only in part whole into view. While exactly one card is chosen, a Move pair (a Move label on a feathered plum pool, like Sort, and two cream arrow keys) stands over the middle of the hand; a tooltip never covers it (a card's tip stands above it), and a press on a tip only closes the tip.
- **Cards.** 41 × 55 art px, corners stepped 4-2-1-1, lit from the upper left; a contact shadow and an ambient shadow on the table; a warm wash, a light sweep when a card scores and a Joker fires, a glint that follows the pointer. Editions are moving light from CSS (`ed-{id}`), Seals sit over the card's edge. A card not found yet (the Collection) is the card's shape in the panels' warm plum with a lit lip and an unlit candle in its window, never a black tile; the Collection's tabs wrap (each count under its name when the plate is narrow), never a hidden scroller.
- **Type.** Jersey 10 for pills, buttons and titles; Tiny5 for small labels and Joker plates; the Ember digit face (S, L, XL, XXL, drawn in code) for every readout; Bricolage Grotesque at 15 px or more for long copy, with coloured keywords.

STORY: The player always knows, at a glance, what the Blind asks (the target), where they stand (the Blind score), what the selection is worth (hand, level, Chips × Mult) and what they have left (hands, discards, money).

SIGNATURE: Lit's payout, driven by the engine's step trace: the played cards arc to the stage and land; each step pops its source (a card hops with a sweep and a number in its colour, a Joker jiggles and hangs a tag, a Boss Blind's emblem pulses) and the Chips and Mult wells roll and flare; then the plaque drops with an XXL bevelled total, ember and pink rings, 32 pixel shards and a flash of the room; the total flies into the Blind score, which counts up. Step kinds it does not know still play as a generic beat (source name and amount).

MOTION: Damped springs sampled into `linear()` (spring, soft, bouncy; one source in `00-core.js`, with the same cubic-bézier fallbacks for the CSS and the Web Animations) for lifts, deals, sorts, pops and the plaque; a strong ease-out, `cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`, for entrances; transform, opacity and filter only. Route changes: a copy of the view that leaves, its cards' light left out, fades in place over the one that arrives (180 ms; 160 ms when only the table's main area changes), which makes its own entrance on the springs; a new visit to the Shop builds its shelves a task after the screen change, input held. Ambient loops keep their own pace and stop in test mode and under reduced motion.
